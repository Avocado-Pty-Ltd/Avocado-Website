The intake path

One number for new enquiries. One clean path into your firm.

Your advertising, Google listing and website point at a dedicated 1300 number. Everything that arrives on it is a potential new client — and it is handled the same way, every time.

Step 01

The enquiry arrives

Google, your website, directories and advertising all point to your dedicated 1300 new-enquiry number.

Step 02

A real person answers

A live Australian Call Concierge team member answers and works through your firm-approved intake script — greeting, practice areas, exclusions, escalation.

Step 03

Your system is updated

We search, then create or update the enquiry, lead or contact in your CRM or PMS — structured note, category, source, owner and follow-up task.

Step 04

You are handed over to

Your nominated contacts receive an immediate structured notification through EzyBiz — not a voicemail, not a loose email.

Existing clients bypass all of this. They keep calling your ordinary direct business number and reach your team as they always have. Call Concierge only ever touches the new-enquiry line.

Then a solicitor decides

Conflict clearance, whether the firm will act, and every question of legal substance stay entirely with your firm. We capture and update. Your firm decides.

What's included

A managed intake desk, not a message-taking service

Every plan includes the number, the people, the script and the systems work. The difference between plans is volume, not quality of handling.

Dedicated 1300 number

One Australian 1300 number for your new-enquiry channel — used on Google, your website, directories and advertising, so enquiry volume and source are finally measurable.

Live Australian answering

A real person, based in Australia, answering during your agreed service window. No offshore call centre, no queue, no automated menu standing between a prospective client and your firm.

Firm-specific greeting and script

Your approved greeting, practice areas, exclusions, the information fields you want captured, and the escalation pathway. Nothing is improvised on your firm's behalf.

Standard CRM/PMS entry

Monthly intake reporting

Call volume, answer rate, intake category, source where known, plan usage and the outcomes you report back to us. You can finally see what the new-enquiry channel is worth.

The difference

Most answering services hand you a message. We hand you a record.

A message in an inbox is still work waiting to be done. By the time you read this month's messages, someone has to re-key them, categorise them, chase them and wonder which ones were lost. Call Concierge closes that loop on the call itself.

  • Enquiries land in the system your firm already runs on
  • Every enquiry categorised and attributed to a source
  • Opposing-party details captured and marked for your conflict check
  • A named owner and a follow-up task, so nothing sits unclaimed
  • Least-privilege access: only the functions and records intake requires

Your firm decides.

Call Concierge is an intake service, not a legal service. Every judgement that belongs to a solicitor stays with your firm, and we have designed the workflow so that it cannot quietly drift.

What Call Concierge does

  • Answers your new-enquiry line as a real Australian person
  • Follows the greeting, questions and exclusions your firm approved
  • Records the enquiry in your CRM or PMS with a structured note
  • Captures opposing-party details and marks them "conflict check required"
  • Escalates on reasonable efforts under your agreed procedure

What Call Concierge never does

  • Give legal advice or any legal assessment of a matter
  • Clear conflicts — that decision is your firm's alone
  • Create a solicitor-client relationship or accept a retainer
  • Quote costs, guarantee an appointment or promise the firm will act
  • Operate as an emergency service — genuine emergencies go to 000

Access to your systems is least-privilege: our team reaches only the functions and records intake requires, under role-based permissions, with record changes auditable. Your firm signs off the greeting, the fields captured, the escalation pathway, the exclusions and the privacy wording before we take a single call — and your firm retains professional responsibility for the matter throughout.

Getting started

Setup is a design exercise, not a sign-up form. We build the script and the workflow with you before the number goes anywhere near your advertising.

Discovery

We map your practice areas, the enquiries you want and the ones you don't, who owns intake, and which CRM or practice management system your firm runs on.

04

Live, then refined

The number goes onto your Google listing, website and advertising. Monthly reporting shows what is arriving, and the script is tuned as your firm learns.
