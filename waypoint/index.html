What law firms ask us first.

Anything else, ask us in the demo.

What counts as a genuine enquiry?

A new person asking about work your firm does. Spam, sales calls, wrong numbers, existing clients calling about a current matter and the same person calling twice in a week are left out. Requests for work you don't do are counted separately, because they show demand you might refer or one day take on. You agree the rules with us at the start, so the number means the same thing every month.

Do we need EzyBiz LEX?

Yes. Waypoint is part of EzyBiz LEX: it's how every call and text is captured and matched to a matter in Smokeball or Clio. Call Concierge is optional, and Waypoint Growth works with your own reception team too.

Where does the rest of the data come from?

Web forms, inboxes, directory and Business Profile messages and your spend per channel come in from your CRM, call tracking or a monthly import. If the records are thin, the first job is putting the tracking in place so next month's figures are complete.

Is this SEO under a new name?

No. SEO is one way to grow leads to matter, and it's part of Waypoint Leadership. For many firms the faster gains are in what happens after someone gets in touch: answering calls, replying sooner, and not losing people before a matter opens. We measure that first.

How do you keep our marketing within the conduct rules?

Your firm is responsible for its advertising, so one of your lawyers signs off every page, profile post and ad before it goes live. Nothing false or misleading, no "best lawyer" claims, "specialist" only where a lawyer holds accreditation, and genuine, consented reviews. This is how we work, not legal advice.

Do you guarantee more matters?

No. Whether an enquiry becomes a matter depends on the person, the matter and your firm's decisions. We commit to accurate measurement, clear reporting on what changed, and doing the agreed fixes well.

Who owns the data and accounts?

You do. Your data stays inside LEX, and tracking numbers, the Business Profile, ad accounts and pages are set up in your firm's name. If you leave, they stay with you.
