Q1What counts as a genuine enquiry?

A new person asking about work you do. Spam, wrong numbers, existing clients and repeat callers within a week are left out. We agree the rules at the start.

Q2Do we need EzyBiz LEX?

Only for Growth and Leadership, which use it to match calls and texts to matters in Smokeball or Clio. Insight works without it. Your numbers can be ported across.

Q3Where does the rest of the data come from?

From your CRM, call tracking or a monthly import. If records are thin, we fix the tracking first.

Q4How do you match an enquiry to a matter?

Each call or text becomes a contact in EzyBiz LEX. When a matter opens in Smokeball or Clio, the contact is attached. Where the link isn't explicit, our inference engine matches by name, phone or email, and keeps improving.

Q5Is this SEO under a new name?

No. SEO is one lever. Often the faster gain is answering sooner, so we measure that first.

Q6How do you keep our marketing within the conduct rules?

A lawyer at your firm signs off every page, post and ad. No false claims, no “best lawyer”, “specialist” only with accreditation. This is how we work, not legal advice.

Q7Do you guarantee more matters?

No. We commit to accurate measurement, clear reporting and doing the agreed work well.

Q8Who owns the data and accounts?

You do. Tracking numbers, profiles, listings, ad accounts and pages are in your firm's name, and stay yours if you leave.
