3 Measurement, attribution and inference

LEX Waypoint counts genuine new enquiries, works out where each one came from (for example search, ads, a directory, social or a referral), and matches it to the matter it became in your practice management system. We agree with you at the start what counts as a genuine enquiry; spam, wrong numbers, existing clients and repeat contacts are left out.

Some enquiries can't be traced. Not every enquiry carries a clear record of its source or of the matter it became. A caller may dial your main number from memory, a tracking tag may be stripped by a browser or an ad platform, a matter may be opened under a different name, phone number or email, or a record may be missing from a connected system. Where an enquiry's source or matter can't be traced directly, we use inference algorithms to make a best guess, based on signals such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, timing, the content of the call or message, and patterns in your firm's other enquiries.

You acknowledge that:

  • an inferred source or match is an estimate, not a record of fact, and may be wrong;
  • inferred results may change when new data arrives, a connected system is updated, or our algorithms improve, so figures for a past period can be revised;
  • the accuracy of traced and inferred results depends on the completeness and accuracy of the data we receive from you and from connected systems; and
  • we take reasonable care in designing and applying our matching and inference, and we will explain on request how a particular figure was arrived at, to the extent we can without disclosing confidential information of other clients.
