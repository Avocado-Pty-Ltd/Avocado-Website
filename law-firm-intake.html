01. The measure

Traffic and rankings don't open files.
Enquiries that become matters do.

Most firms can say how many calls came in last month. Fewer can say how many became matters, where those people found the firm, or how many rang out. Leads to matter answers all three, on one page.

We count enquiries and matters for the same period, and link each opened matter back to the enquiry it came from wherever the records allow. Where they don't yet, fixing that is part of the work.

What isn't a genuine enquiry
  • Spam and sales calls. Robocalls, marketing pitches, recruiters and wrong numbers.
  • Work you don't do. A conveyancing enquiry at a criminal practice is real, but it can't become your matter. We count it separately so you can see the demand.
  • Existing clients. Calls about a current matter are client service, not new enquiries.
  • Duplicates. The same person calling, then filling in a form, counts once.

You agree the rules with us at the start, so the number means the same thing every month.

02. Where leads leak

Three ways to break it down.
Each one points to a different fix.

Source

Where did they find you?

SEOBusiness ProfileGoogle AdsReferralsDirectories

Some sources send plenty of enquiries that rarely become matters. Others send few, but most of them sign. Without a known source on every lead, you can't tell which is which, and spend follows guesswork.

So: tracking numbers, UTM and gclid capture on forms, and a source field on every enquiry, including referrals and directories such as LawConnect.

Contact method

How did they get in touch?

CallsWeb formsEmailsMessages

A call can be answered on the spot. A form can sit in a shared inbox over a weekend. A Business Profile message can go unseen because nobody knows it exists. Each path has its own leaks.

So: we measure each path separately, then fix the weak ones: shorter forms, clear tap-to-call on mobile, one owner for every inbox.

Response

What happened next?

Response timeMissed callsCallbacksDrop-off

People looking for a lawyer often contact more than one firm. Slow replies, missed calls with no callback, and enquiries that stall between the first conversation and an opened matter all show up here.

So: response time and missed-call reports by hour and day, agreed callback targets, and a reason recorded when an enquiry doesn't proceed.

04. How it works

Measure, fix, grow.
In that order.

Step 01 · Measure

Find your number

We import what you already have and set the counting rules with you.

  • Enquiry log built from calls, forms, inboxes and your matter list
  • Genuine enquiries separated from spam and out-of-scope requests
  • A first leads-to-matter figure, by source and contact method
Step 02 · Fix

Close the biggest leaks

One or two fixes at a time, starting with the largest, so you can see what each one changed.

  • Call handling, missed-call text-back and callback targets
  • Clearer contact paths and shorter forms
  • Source tracking on every lead
Step 03 · Grow

Add demand you can convert

Once intake holds up, we add enquiries through the sources that turn into matters.

  • Business Profile and review requests
  • Money pages, then the full SEO programme
  • Google Ads if it earns its place on cost per matter
Compliance by design

Built around the rules
Australian lawyers work under.

Your firm is responsible for its advertising, so one of your lawyers signs off every page, profile post and ad before it goes live. We build that step into the work.

  • ASCR rule 36. Nothing false, misleading, deceptive or offensive. No "best lawyer in Sydney" and no implied guarantees.
  • Specialist claims. "Accredited specialist" appears only where a lawyer holds accreditation from their state law society or institute.
  • Personal injury. In NSW, Queensland and the Northern Territory, personal injury pages and ads follow the state limits on advertising those services.
  • Australian Consumer Law. Reviews and testimonials are genuine and consented. Review requests go to every client, never only the happy ones, and never for a reward.
  • Client confidentiality. Enquiry logs hold personal information, so reports use counts and categories, not names. Case results are de-identified unless the client has given written consent.
  • Call tracking and recording. Set up with your firm so callers are told what they need to be told and your privacy policy covers it.

This is how we work, not legal advice. Your firm should confirm its own obligations with its state's legal services regulator or law society.

05. Questions

What law firms ask us first.

What counts as a genuine enquiry?

A new person asking about work your firm does. We leave out spam, sales calls, wrong numbers, existing clients calling about a current matter, duplicates, and requests for work you don't do. Out-of-scope requests are still counted separately, because they show demand you might want to refer or one day take on. You agree the rules with us at the start.

Where does the enquiry data come from?

From what you already have: your phone system's call records, website form submissions, the inboxes enquiries go to, directory and Business Profile messages, and your matter list from Smokeball, Clio, LEAP or a spreadsheet. If the records are thin, the first job is putting the tracking in place so next month's figures are complete.

Is this SEO under a new name?

No. SEO is one way to grow leads to matter, and it's part of Intake Leadership. But for many firms the faster gains are in what happens after someone gets in touch: answering calls, replying sooner, and not losing people between the first conversation and an opened matter. We measure that first.

Can we start with Intake Insight only?

Yes. Insight is built to stand on its own. You get the dashboard, the audit and a monthly report naming what to fix. Your team can act on it, or you can move to Growth when you want us to do the fixing.

Do we have to use Call Concierge or EzyBiz LEX?

No. They're our own products and they connect to Smokeball and Clio, so they're often the quickest fix for missed calls. But Intake Growth works with your own reception team or another provider, as long as calls and outcomes are recorded.

How does Google Ads fit in?

It's a separate add-on, available with any plan. We manage the campaigns and report them on cost per matter, the same way as every other source, so you can compare ads with search, referrals and directories on equal terms.

Do you guarantee more matters?

No. Whether an enquiry becomes a matter depends on the person, the matter and your firm's decisions. We commit to accurate measurement, clear reporting on what changed, and doing the agreed fixes well.

Who owns the data and accounts?

You do. The dashboard data, tracking numbers, Business Profile, ad accounts and pages are set up in your firm's name. If you leave, everything stays with you.

What does the free intake review include?

A 30-minute call, then a short written summary: how enquiries reach you today, where they're most likely leaking, what data you already have to measure leads to matter, and which plan, if any, we'd recommend.
